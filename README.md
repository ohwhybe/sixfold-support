# Sixfold: Support

**Sixfold: Roll & Match**, a dice puzzle for iPhone.

## Get help

Open an issue: **https://github.com/ohwhybe/sixfold-support/issues**

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version (Settings > General > About), what happened, and what you
did just before.

## Questions

**How do I play?**
In the game, open Settings (the gear) and choose How to play.

**How many daily puzzles are free?**
Your first 7 daily puzzles are free. After that, one purchase unlocks the daily puzzle: a new puzzle every day, no
subscription. The endless game is free on every board size.

**I bought the unlock, but the daily puzzle is locked.**
Open Settings and choose Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.
On a new iPhone, Restore purchase brings it back too.

**Can my family use the unlock?**
Yes, the unlock works with Family Sharing. If it doesn't appear on a family member's iPhone, they can choose
Restore purchase in Settings.

**If I delete the app, do my free puzzles come back?**
No. The count is kept on your iPhone so it survives reinstalling the app.

**The moving background bothers me.**
Open Settings and turn off Background motion. Sixfold also respects iOS Reduce Motion.

## Privacy

Sixfold collects no data. Read the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
