# Privacy Policy: Sixfold

Last updated: October 2026

Sixfold is built so there is nothing to collect. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and
no tracking of any kind. Sixfold makes no network requests of its own, and there is no Sixfold server.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your saved run, your best scores, your daily puzzle results and streak, and your settings are stored on your iPhone
and never leave it. If you save an end-of-run card to Photos, iOS asks your permission first, and Sixfold only adds
that one picture; it never reads your photos.

## Your free daily puzzles

To count your 7 free daily puzzles, Sixfold keeps the numbers of the days you have played in your iPhone's keychain,
so the count survives deleting and reinstalling the app on the same phone. It holds nothing else, it is not synced
through iCloud Keychain, and it is never sent anywhere. An encrypted backup of your iPhone may carry it to a new
phone.

## Purchases

The daily puzzle unlock is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account;
Sixfold only learns whether the unlock is owned. Apple's privacy policy covers purchases.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
